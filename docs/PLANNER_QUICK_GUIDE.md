# Travel Mood Board Planner - Quick Guide

## 🎯 What Changed?

**Before:** Static image gallery  
**After:** Interactive travel planning system

---

## 🚀 How to Use

### 1️⃣ **Click Any Category Card**
- Nature, Food, Architecture, Beaches, Culture, Cafes, Adventure, or Nightlife
- Opens detailed planning modal

### 2️⃣ **Explore Category Details**
Modal shows:
- Why this category is popular
- Top 3 recommended places
- Estimated costs
- Best time to visit
- Recommended duration
- Category tags

### 3️⃣ **Set Your Priority**
Choose one:
- 🟢 **Must Visit** - Essential experiences
- 🔵 **Nice to Visit** - Optional but recommended  
- ⚪ **Skip** - Not interested

### 4️⃣ **Add Places to Itinerary**
- Click "Add" button on any place
- Toast confirms: "Added [Place] to itinerary!"
- Button changes to "Added ✓"

### 5️⃣ **Open Your Itinerary**
- Click "My Itinerary" button (top-right)
- Sidebar slides in showing:
  - Total activities
  - Total budget
  - Estimated days
  - Progress score
  - Travel insights
  - AI recommendations
  - All saved activities

### 6️⃣ **Manage Your Plan**
- Remove activities (trash icon)
- View budget breakdown
- Track progress
- Get smart suggestions
